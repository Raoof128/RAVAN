# Tide Almanac

A 90-second original photographic study made for the RAVĀN sample library. A figure measures the tide at first light, the view pulls back to the coastline, and the film returns to the waterline. The edit changes at 00:30 and 01:00, with caption cues spanning each transition for seek tests.

The dawn image was generated for this app. The other shot uses the project's original coast artwork, documented in the repository's design-art-manifest.json. The app poster is a crop of the dawn image. The RAVĀN app icon and website share an original copper film-ribbon emblem generated for this project. The layered source lives in Ravan/RavanRibbon.icon. The app bundles the regular weight of the full Cormorant Garamond font, including the Ā in RAVĀN. Its SIL Open Font License ships beside the font. [Google Fonts source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond)

The MP4 contains H.264 video, an original synthesised surf soundtrack, and embedded English and Persian MPEG-4 timed text. English.srt and Persian.srt are the editable caption sources. There is no third-party film footage, music or stock soundtrack. The MP4 can be copied to Files for a local playback test or used as the sole film on an isolated test server.
